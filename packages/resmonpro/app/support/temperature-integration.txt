Hardware temperature collection is built into Resource Manager Pro.

service.py supervises monitor.py and temperature/server.py under the package user.
The temperature worker listens only on 127.0.0.1:39188 and stores its SQLite database,
backups and logs under /var/apps/resmonpro/var/temperature.

Legacy Thermoboard data must be migrated once before the new worker starts.
Use scripts/migrate_thermoboard.py from the source project and retain its backup.
After verifying history and continued sampling, the legacy FPK may be uninstalled.
